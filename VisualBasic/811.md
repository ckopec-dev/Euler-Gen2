# Euler Problem 811 Solution in Visual Basic

Euler Problem 811 asks us to find the sum of all positive integers n ≤ 10^12 such that n has exactly 12 divisors and n is divisible by 12.

Let me break this down:
- n ≤ 10^12
- n has exactly 12 divisors
- n is divisible by 12

First, I need to understand when a number has exactly 12 divisors. If n = p₁^a₁ × p₂^a₂ × ... × pₖ^aₖ, then the number of divisors is (a₁+1)(a₂+1)...(aₖ+1) = 12.

The possible factorizations of 12 are:
- 12 = 12 (n = p^11)
- 12 = 6 × 2 (n = p^5 × q^1)
- 12 = 4 × 3 (n = p^3 × q^2)
- 12 = 3 × 2 × 2 (n = p^2 × q^1 × r^1)
- 12 = 2 × 2 × 2 × 2 (n = p^1 × q^1 × r^1 × s^1)

But we also need n to be divisible by 12 = 2² × 3, so n must include at least 2² × 3 in its prime factorization.

        ' We can have p = 2, q = 3, r = other prime or similar arrangements

                    End If
