# Euler Problem 226 Solution in Visual Basic

Euler Problem 226 asks us to find the area of the region inside the circle of radius 1/2 centered at (1/2, 0) and outside the two circles of radius 1/4 centered at (1/4, 0) and (3/4, 0).

Let me solve this step by step:

```vb
Module Problem226
    Sub Main()
        ' Calculate the area of the region inside the circle of radius 1/2
        ' centered at (1/2, 0) and outside the two circles of radius 1/4
        ' centered at (1/4, 0) and (3/4, 0)
        
        Dim radius1 As Double = 0.5
        Dim radius2 As Double = 0.25
        
        ' Area of the large circle (radius 1/2)
        Dim areaLargeCircle As Double = Math.PI * radius1 * radius1
        
        ' Area of two small circles (radius 1/4 each)
        Dim areaSmallCircles As Double = 2 * Math.PI * radius2 * radius2
        
        ' The region we want is the area of the large circle minus
        ' the area of the two small circles
        Dim result As Double = areaLargeCircle - areaSmallCircles
        
        Console.WriteLine("Area of the region: " & result.ToString("F10"))
        Console.WriteLine("Exact value: " & (Math.PI * 0.25).ToString("F10"))
        
        ' For more precision, we can calculate it exactly
        ' Area = π*(1/2)² - 2*π*(1/4)² = π/4 - 2*π/16 = π/4 - π/8 = π/8
        Dim exactResult As Double = Math.PI / 8
        
        Console.WriteLine("Exact result: π/8 = " & exactResult.ToString("F10"))
        Console.WriteLine("Decimal approximation: " & (exactResult).ToString("F10"))
    End Sub
End Module
```
