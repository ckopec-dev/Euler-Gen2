## Mathematical Analysis

For a spiral of the form r = a * e^(-bθ), where:
- r is the radius at angle θ
- a and b are constants
- The car starts at radius R and spirals to the center

The arc length formula for a polar curve r = f(θ) is:
L = ∫√(r² + (dr/dθ)²) dθ

For r = R * e^(-kθ), where k is a constant:
- dr/dθ = -k * R * e^(-kθ)
- r² + (dr/dθ)² = R² * e^(-2kθ) + k² * R² * e^(-2kθ) = R² * e^(-2kθ) * (1 + k²)

So the arc length becomes:
L = ∫R * e^(-kθ) * √(1 + k²) dθ = R * √(1 + k²) * ∫e^(-kθ) dθ
