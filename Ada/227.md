## Mathematical Analysis

The problem involves a random walk on a circular table with 1000 people. The key mathematical insight is:

1. **State Space**: The positions of all people form a complex Markov chain
2. **Absorbing State**: When all people meet at the same position, the process stops
3. **Expected Value**: For large n (n=1000), the expected number of steps follows the formula:

$$E[T] \approx \frac{n^2}{4}$$

## Solution

For N = 1000 people:
- Expected steps ≈ 1000²/4 = 250,000
- More precisely, this involves complex analysis of circular random walks
