# Euler Problem 317 Solution in Ada

This problem asks us to find the volume of the region enclosed by the surface z = 10e^(-x² - y²) and the plane z = 0, where x and y are constrained to the region [0,2] × [0,2].

The volume is given by the double integral:
V = ∫∫[0,2] ∫[0,2] 10e^(-x² - y²) dx dy

Let me solve this step by step:

## Mathematical Approach

Since the integrand factors as a product of functions of x and y, we can separate the integral:
V = 10 ∫[0,2] e^(-x²) dx × ∫[0,2] e^(-y²) dy

The integral ∫[0,2] e^(-t²) dt is related to the error function, but we'll compute it numerically.

      Num_Samples : constant Integer := 10000;

      Sum         : Float := 0.0;
