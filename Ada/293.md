I'll solve Euler Problem 293, which asks for the number of ways to write a number as a sum of powers of 2 with specific constraints.

## Problem Understanding

Euler Problem 293 asks: Find the number of ways to write a positive integer n as a sum of powers of 2 such that:
- Each power of 2 can be used at most twice
- The representation is unique (no two consecutive powers of 2 can both be used)
