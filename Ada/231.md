This Ada solution implements the key mathematical principle for solving this problem:

1. **Legendre's Formula**: For a prime p and integer n, the highest power of p that divides n! is given by:
   $v_p(n!) = \sum_{i=1}^{\infty} \lfloor \frac{n}{p^i} \rfloor$

2. **Binomial Coefficient Prime Factorization**: 
   For C(N,K) = N!/(K!(N-K)!), the power of prime p in C(N,K) is:
   $v_p(C(N,K)) = v_p(N!) - v_p(K!) - v_p((N-K)!)$

3. **Sum of Prime Factors**: The sum we want is:
   $\sum_{p \leq N} p \cdot v_p(C(N,K))$

The algorithm uses the Sieve of Eratosthenes to find all primes up to 20,000,000 and then computes the contribution of each prime to the final sum.
