- A car travels around the track with constant speed v = 20 m/s
- The car's path is described by the parametric equations of a circle
- We need to find the total distance traveled in 10^9 seconds

   v : constant Float := 20.0;          -- Speed in m/s
   T : constant Float := 1.0E9;         -- Time in seconds
   
   -- Calculate the circumference of the circular track

with Ada.Text_IO; use Ada.Text_IO;
with Ada.Numerics.Elementary_Functions; use Ada.Numerics.Elementary_Functions;

procedure Euler_314 is

   Radius : constant Float := 1000.0;     -- meters
   Speed : constant Float := 20.0;        -- m/s
   Time : constant Float := 1.0E9;        -- seconds
   
   -- Calculate circumference of the circular track
   Circumference : constant Float := 2.0 * Ada.Numerics.Pi * Radius;
   
   -- Calculate time for one complete revolution
   Time_Per_Revolution : constant Float := Circumference / Speed;
   
   -- Calculate number of complete revolutions
   Complete_Revolutions : constant Integer := 
      Integer(Time / Time_Per_Revolution);
   
   -- Calculate remaining time after complete revolutions
   Remaining_Time : constant Float := Time - Float(Complete_Revolutions) * Time_Per_Revolution;
   
   -- Total distance = distance from complete revolutions + remaining distance
   Total_Distance : constant Float := 
      Float(Complete_Revolutions) * Circumference + (Remaining_Time * Speed);
   
begin
   Put_Line("Euler Problem 314 Solution:");
   Put_Line("Track radius: " & Radius'Img & " meters");
   Put_Line("Car speed: " & Speed'Img & " m/s");

   Put_Line("Final Answer: " & Integer(Total_Distance)'Img);

The key insight for this problem is:
1. The car travels in a circular path with radius R = 1000 m
2. Speed v = 20 m/s is constant
3. The circumference of the track = 2πR = 2000π meters
4. Time to complete one circle = 2000π/20 = 100π seconds
5. In 10^9 seconds, the number of complete circles = 10^9 / (100π) 
6. The total distance is the number of complete circles times the circumference plus any remaining distance

The answer should be approximately 10^9 × 20 = 2 × 10^10 meters, since the car travels at constant speed for the entire time period.
