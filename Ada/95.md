# Euler Problem 95 Solution in Ada

## Problem Statement
The proper divisors of a number are all the divisors excluding the number itself. For example, the proper divisors of 28 are 1, 2, 4, 7, and 14. As the sum of these divisors is equal to 28, we call it a perfect number.

The aliquot sequence of a number can be generated by repeatedly summing the proper divisors of a number. For example, starting with 28:
- 28 → 1 + 2 + 4 + 7 + 14 = 28 (perfect number)
- 12 → 1 + 2 + 3 + 4 + 6 = 16
- 16 → 1 + 2 + 4 + 8 = 15
- 15 → 1 + 3 + 5 = 9
- 9 → 1 + 3 = 4
- 4 → 1 + 2 = 3
- 3 → 1 = 1

The longest aliquot sequence with a member less than one million is generated from the number 14316.

Find the smallest number in the longest aliquot sequence with a member less than one million.
