# Euler Problem 772 Solution in Kotlin

## Problem Statement
The problem asks us to find the sum of all positive integers n ≤ 10^12 such that n has exactly 12 divisors and n is divisible by 12.

## Analysis
For a number n to have exactly 12 divisors, we need to consider the divisor function. If n = p₁^a₁ × p₂^a₂ × ... × pₖ^aₖ, then the number of divisors is (a₁ + 1)(a₂ + 1)...(aₖ + 1) = 12.

The possible factorizations of 12 are:
- 12 = 12 (n = p₁¹¹)
- 12 = 6 × 2 (n = p₁⁵ × p₂¹)
- 12 = 4 × 3 (n = p₁³ × p₂²)
- 12 = 3 × 2 × 2 (n = p₁² × p₂¹ × p₃¹)
- 12 = 2 × 2 × 2 × 2 (n = p₁¹ × p₂¹ × p₃¹ × p₄¹)

Since n must be divisible by 12 = 2² × 3, we must include at least 2² and 3¹ in our prime factorization.

## Solution Approach
1. Generate all valid combinations of prime powers that result in exactly 12 divisors
2. Ensure each combination includes at least 2² and 3¹
3. For each valid combination, generate all numbers ≤ 10¹²
4. Sum all valid numbers
