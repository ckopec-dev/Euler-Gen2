I'll solve Euler Problem 842, which asks for the sum of all integers k such that the number of divisors of k is exactly 2023.

## Problem Analysis

First, let me understand what we're looking for:
- We need to find all integers k where d(k) = 2023 (number of divisors = 2023)

## Key Mathematical Insight

If a number k has the prime factorization k = p₁^a₁ × p₂^a₂ × ... × pₙ^aₙ, then:
d(k) = (a₁ + 1)(a₂ + 1)...(aₙ + 1)

So we need to find all ways to write 2023 as a product of integers ≥ 2, then find the corresponding k values.

## Prime Factorization of 2023

Let's find the prime factorization of 2023:
- 2023 = 7 × 289 = 7 × 17² = 7 × 17 × 17

So 2023 = 7 × 17²

The divisors of 2023 are: 1, 7, 17, 119, 289, 2023

## Possible Factorizations

We need to express 2023 as a product of factors (each ≥ 2) representing (a₁ + 1)(a₂ + 1)...(aₙ + 1).

The possible factorizations are:
1. 2023 = 2023 (one prime factor)
2. 2023 = 119 × 17 (two prime factors)
3. 2023 = 289 × 7 (two prime factors)
4. 2023 = 7 × 17 × 17 (three prime factors)
