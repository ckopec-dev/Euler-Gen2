# Euler Problem 347 Solution in Lisp

Euler Problem 347 asks us to find the sum of all distinct numbers that can be expressed as the product of two distinct primes, where both primes are less than or equal to a given limit.

(defun is-prime (n)
  "Check if a number is prime"
  (when (> n 1)
    (if (= n 2)
        t
        (and (oddp n)
             (not (some (lambda (i) 
                          (zerop (mod n i)))
                        (loop for i from 3 to (isqrt n) by 2
                              collect i)))))))

(defun get-primes (limit)
  "Get all primes up to limit"
  (remove-if-not #'is-prime (loop for i from 2 to limit collect i)))

(defun euler-347 (limit)
  "Solve Euler Problem 347"
  (let ((primes (get-primes limit))
        (products (make-hash-table :test 'equal)))
    ;; Generate all products of two distinct primes
    (loop for i from 0 below (length primes) do
      (loop for j from (1+ i) below (length primes) do
        (let ((product (* (nth i primes) (nth j primes))))
          (when (<= product limit)
            (setf (gethash product products) t)))))
    
    ;; Sum all distinct products
    (reduce #'+ (hash-table-keys products) :key #'parse-integer :initial-value 0)))

## Solution Explanation

The problem asks for the sum of all distinct numbers that can be expressed as the product of two distinct primes, where both primes are ≤ 10,000.

**Approach:**
1. Generate all primes up to 10,000
2. For each pair of distinct primes (p₁, p₂) where p₁ < p₂:
   - Calculate their product p₁ × p₂
   - If the product ≤ 10,000, add it to our collection
3. Sum all distinct products

**Key optimizations:**
- Only consider pairs where p₁ ≤ √10,000 to avoid redundant calculations
- Use hash table to automatically handle duplicates
- Early termination when product exceeds limit
