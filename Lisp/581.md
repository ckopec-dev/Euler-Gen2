# Euler Problem 581 Solution in Lisp

Euler Problem 581 asks us to find the sum of all numbers n ≤ 10^11 such that n is divisible by 10 and the number of divisors of n is divisible by 10.

Let me break this down:
- n ≤ 10^11
- n is divisible by 10
- τ(n) (number of divisors) is divisible by 10

## Approach

The key insight is that for a number n = p₁^a₁ × p₂^a₂ × ... × pₖ^aₖ, the number of divisors is τ(n) = (a₁+1)(a₂+1)...(aₖ+1).

Since n must be divisible by 10, we have n = 2^a × 5^b × m where m is not divisible by 2 or 5, and a,b ≥ 1.

So τ(n) = (a+1)(b+1) × τ(m) where τ(m) is the number of divisors of m.

For τ(n) to be divisible by 10, we need (a+1)(b+1) × τ(m) ≡ 0 (mod 10).

Since 10 = 2 × 5, we need the product to be divisible by both 2 and 5.
