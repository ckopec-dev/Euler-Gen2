# Euler Problem 159 Solution in Lisp

Euler Problem 159 asks us to find the maximum digital sum of a number and its divisors, where we consider all numbers from 1 to 1000000.

Let me break this down:
- For each number n, we calculate the digital sum of n
- We also calculate the digital sum of all divisors of n
- We want the maximum digital sum among all numbers and their divisors

Here's my solution in Common Lisp:

  "Calculate the digital sum of a number"
  (if (zerop n)
      0

  "Calculate the sum of all divisors of n"
  (let ((sum 0))
    (loop for i from 1 to (isqrt n)
          when (zerop (mod n i))
            do (progn
                 (incf sum i)
                 (when (not (= i (floor n i)))

    sum))

    (let ((digit-sums (make-array (1+ limit) :element-type 'fixnum :initial-element 0)))
