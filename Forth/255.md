## Mathematical Solution Explanation

The key insight for Euler Problem 255 is recognizing the pattern:

1. **Digital Root Pattern**: The digital root of numbers 1-9 is 1,2,3,4,5,6,7,8,9, which sums to 45
2. **Repeating Pattern**: This pattern repeats for every 9 consecutive numbers (10-18, 19-27, etc.)
3. **Mathematical Formula**: For a range 1 to n:
   - Complete groups of 9: `n/9` groups, each contributes 45
   - Remaining numbers: `n%9` numbers, whose digital roots sum to `r*(r+1)/2` where `r = n%9`
