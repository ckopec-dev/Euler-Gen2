# Euler Problem 202 Solution in COBOL

Euler Problem 202 asks: "How many different ways can we arrange the digits 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9 to form a number that is divisible by 9?"

Let me solve this step by step in COBOL.

## Analysis
- We need to arrange digits 1-9 to form numbers divisible by 9
- A number is divisible by 9 if the sum of its digits is divisible by 9
- Sum of digits 1+2+3+4+5+6+7+8+9 = 45
- Since 45 is divisible by 9, ANY arrangement of these digits will form a number divisible by 9
- This is simply a permutation problem: 9! = 362,880

## COBOL Solution

```cobol
       IDENTIFICATION DIVISION.
       PROGRAM-ID. EULER202.
       AUTHOR. COBOL SOLUTION.

       ENVIRONMENT DIVISION.
       INPUT-OUTPUT SECTION.
       FILE-CONTROL.

       DATA DIVISION.
       FILE SECTION.

       WORKING-STORAGE SECTION.
       01 FACTORIAL-RESULT    PIC 9(10) VALUE 1.
       01 I                   PIC 9(3) VALUE 1.
       01 J                   PIC 9(3) VALUE 1.
       01 TOTAL-WAYS          PIC 9(10) VALUE 0.
       01 DIGITS              PIC 9(1) VALUE 9.
       01 TEMP-RESULT         PIC 9(10) VALUE 1.
       01 DISPLAY-RESULT      PIC Z(9)9.

       PROCEDURE DIVISION.
       MAIN-LOGIC.
           PERFORM CALCULATE-FACTORIAL
           DISPLAY "Number of arrangements divisible by 9: " TOTAL-WAYS
           STOP RUN.

       CALCULATE-FACTORIAL.
           MOVE 1 TO FACTORIAL-RESULT
           MOVE 1 TO I
           PERFORM UNTIL I > 9
               MULTIPLY FACTORIAL-RESULT BY I GIVING FACTORIAL-RESULT
               ADD 1 TO I
           END-PERFORM
           MOVE FACTORIAL-RESULT TO TOTAL-WAYS
           GO TO END-FACTORIAL.

       END-FACTORIAL.
           EXIT.

       END PROGRAM EULER202.
```

           PERFORM VARYING COUNT FROM 1 BY 1 UNTIL COUNT > 9

## Explanation

The solution works as follows:

1. **Problem Analysis**: 
   - We have digits 1,2,3,4,5,6,7,8,9
   - Sum = 1+2+3+4+5+6+7+8+9 = 45
   - Since 45 is divisible by 9, any arrangement of these digits will form a number divisible by 9
   - This is a permutation problem: 9! arrangements

2. **COBOL Implementation**:
   - Initialize factorial result to 1
   - Loop from 1 to 9, multiplying each number to get 9!
   - Display the result

3. **Result**:
   - 9! = 9 × 8 × 7 × 6 × 5 × 4 × 3 × 2 × 1 = 362,880

## Final Answer

The number of different ways to arrange the digits 1,2,3,4,5,6,7,8,9 to form a number divisible by 9 is **362,880**.

This is because any arrangement of the digits 1-9 will result in a number divisible by 9 (since the sum of digits 45 is divisible by 9), and there are 9! = 362,880 such arrangements.
