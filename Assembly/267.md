# Euler Problem 267 Solution in Assembly

I'll solve Euler Problem 267, which involves finding the optimal strategy for a betting game with logarithmic returns.

## Problem Understanding

Euler Problem 267 asks us to find the optimal fraction of our bankroll to bet in a game where:
- We start with £1
- Each round we win with probability 0.6 and lose with probability 0.4
- When we win, we gain the amount we bet; when we lose, we lose the amount we bet
- We play 1000 rounds
- We want to maximize the probability of ending with at least £1,000,000

## Mathematical Analysis

The optimal fraction to bet is given by the Kelly Criterion:
- f* = p - q = 0.6 - 0.4 = 0.2

This means we should bet 20% of our current bankroll each round.
