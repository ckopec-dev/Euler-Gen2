# Euler Problem 144 Solution in LMC

Euler Problem 144 asks us to find the number of reflections a laser beam makes before exiting an elliptical mirror system.

## Problem Analysis

The problem involves:
- An elliptical mirror with equation x² + 4y² = 100
- A laser beam starting at (0,10.1) and hitting the ellipse at (1.4,-9.6)
- Finding how many times the beam reflects before exiting
- The beam exits when it hits the ellipse at a point where y > 10
