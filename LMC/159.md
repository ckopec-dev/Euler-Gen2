This LMC program calculates the maximum digital sum of numbers of the form a^b where both a and b are less than 100. The approach:
1. Loop through all pairs (a,b) from 1 to 99
2. For each pair, calculate a^b
3. Calculate the digital sum of the result
4. Keep track of the maximum digital sum found

The digital sum calculation uses repeated division by 10 to extract each digit and sum them up.
